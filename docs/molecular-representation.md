# Molecular representation

`Compound` stores names, optional formula, molecular weight, structure identifiers, SMILES, InChI, InChIKey, chemical classes, and provenance. `CompoundMapping` separates `COMPOUND_PRESENT`, `SENSORY_RELEVANT`, and `CAUSALLY_ESTABLISHED`; presence never implies sensory relevance or causality. Concentration retains basis, statistic, matrix, method, and units.
