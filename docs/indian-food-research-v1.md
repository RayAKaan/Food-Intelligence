# INDIAN_FOOD_RESEARCH_V1

## Status: pending authorized ELR snapshot

The research track contains no raw recipe records yet. ELR-1000 is the only current primary admission candidate: 1,073 community-contributed recipes, ten endangered Indic languages, explicit consent claims, and KPL BY-NC-SA-FS 1.0. Hugging Face requires login and acceptance of contact-sharing conditions before files can be accessed. No credentials were available, so no raw ELR data was downloaded.

INDB is not admitted wholesale. ASC and BFP are cookbook-derived and OSR is website-derived; all remain source-specific review candidates. The accepted research corpus will preserve source language, source names, translations, cultural metadata, and raw record identifiers. No research data may enter the commercial snapshot.

## Admission contract

Each record must carry `dataset`, `source_id`, `source_record_id`, `license_status`, `provenance_confidence`, and `admission_status`. A missing quantity, state, temperature, duration, or process is stored as NULL/UNKNOWN, never inferred silently.

## Current scientific use

Once ELR is authorized and snapshotted, it can support multilingual ingredient-resolution evaluation, source-aware recipe retrieval, co-occurrence pairing baselines, recipe-group leakage-safe splits, and corpus-relative novelty. It cannot by itself establish taste, odor, chemical compatibility, deliciousness, safety, or population representativeness.
