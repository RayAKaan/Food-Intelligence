# Corpus coverage — current indexed corpus

No claim of representativeness is made.

## Research candidate coverage

ELR-1000's repository metadata reports Assamese, Bodo, Ho, Kaman-Mishmi, Khasi, Khortha, Meitei, Mundari, Sadri, and Santhali, with 1,073 recipes. These are candidate-level statistics, not admitted-record statistics, because the files require authenticated access.

## Commercial admitted coverage

Sangat's admitted seed record is a North Indian dish (`dal-tadka`) with Hindi and English names. This is a single-source, single-record supplement and provides no basis for regional coverage claims.

## Bias and limitations

The future research snapshot will be community- and language-rich but not necessarily geographically or socially representative of India. The commercial snapshot is intentionally tiny. Report observed indexed coverage, not “Indian cuisine” as a whole.
