# Open corpus construction plans

## Plan A — commercial-safe

**Current defensible recipe count: 0.** Sangat Food DB is the only strong commercial-open recipe-adjacent source located, but its current repository is a nutrition/dish metadata seed targeting approximately 200 dishes and does not expose full structured instructions. It should contribute an optional commercial-safe dish/nutrition layer, not satisfy the recipe corpus target. Expected ingredient count is not yet measurable from the repository scaffold; regional target is North/West/South/East, with current seed evidence strongest for North.

**Gap:** at least several thousand full recipes with explicit first-party or contributor rights.

## Plan B — research corpus

**Minimum defensible candidate currently identified: ELR-1000, 1,073 community-consented recipes.** It provides 10 endangered Indic languages, Eastern Indian coverage, text/images/audio, cultural context, and community provenance. It is research/noncommercial under KPL BY-NC-SA-FS and cannot enter the commercial track. INDB may add up to 1,014 records only after source-class rights review; do not count them yet. A combined research corpus could therefore be approximately 1,073–2,087 records, but overlap, source rights, and schema compatibility must be audited before claiming the upper count.

This would remain below 6,000 and should be framed as a culturally rich research benchmark rather than a nationally representative corpus.

## Plan C — permission expansion

Add Mendeley/Archana-derived 6,871-row data, INDoRI 5,187 rows, or EmTpro01 only after written/source rights are established. These are high-volume candidates but not open-safe today.

## Recommended strategy

Admit ELR-1000 to the research candidate manifest after its gated access terms are accepted and a snapshot is acquired. Use Sangat as a commercial-open nutrition/portion supplement. Audit INDB by source prefix. Keep Archana-derived, INDoRI, and EmTpro01 outside all manifests until rights are resolved.

Do not claim a 6,000-recipe corpus in Phase 1B.
